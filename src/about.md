# About Human Science Network

Human Science Network is a free, curated directory of scholarly conferences, congresses and workshops across the human sciences. We started with psychology and are expanding to anthropology, sociology, behavioral economics and decision science, linguistics, education research, and health and medical science.

Our aim is simple: one place where researchers can find **legitimate** meetings in their field, with dates and submission deadlines they can trust.

<h2 id="accuracy">How we keep listings accurate</h2>


- Every listing links to the organizer's official page, and we record when we last checked it.
- We never carry dates forward or guess them. If the next edition hasn't been announced, the event is marked **"to be confirmed"**.
- Events move to [Past events](/past/) automatically once they end.
- We recheck upcoming events and deadlines every month, and every organizer once a year.

<h2 id="criteria">Inclusion criteria</h2>


An event is listed only if **all** of the following apply:

1. **Legitimate academic host.** A scholarly society or professional association, a university or research institute, a recognized research network, or a public research body. Professional event companies may help run a meeting, but an academic body must own it.
2. **Scholarly focus.** The event is about research in one or more human-science fields, with a named programme or scientific committee of identifiable researchers.
3. **Real peer review.** There is a stated review process for submissions, with realistic timelines.
4. **Transparency.** The official site states dates, location or format, the organizing body, contact details, registration fees and the refund policy.
5. **Verifiable.** We can confirm the details on the host's own website or official channels.

<h2 id="screened">What we screen out</h2>


We don't list events that show signs of being commercial or pseudo-academic, including:

- for-profit "conference series" that run many generic, multi-discipline events,
- guaranteed or very fast acceptance, or pay-to-present without review,
- promises of publication in indexed journals for a fee,
- committees or venues that can't be verified, hidden fees, or aggressive unsolicited invitations.

Paid placement never buys a listing. Any sponsored content will be clearly labelled and must still meet the criteria above.

## Privacy

This site uses no analytics, no tracking scripts, no cookies and no third-party fonts. Forms send only what you type into them.
