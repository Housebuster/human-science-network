Human Science Network is a curated directory of scholarly conferences and workshops across the human sciences. We have two goals:

1. Provide a place where researchers, scholars, and practitioners can find a list of legitimate meetings to discuss scientific approaches to human behavior. Listings include both on-site and online meetings.
2. Expand the capacity of the human sciences worldwide by connecting researchers across countries and cultures.

Our current listings focus mostly on psychology but we welcome entries from other fields where human behavior is a major theme of the event. 

Want to list your event? Click on "Submit an Event".

## Privacy

Currently the site uses no analytics, no tracking scripts, no cookies and no third-party fonts. 

## Corrections

If you spot an error, please [let us know](/contact/?topic=correction). Organizers can [submit an event](/submit/) for review.
